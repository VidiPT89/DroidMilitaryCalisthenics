# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

DroidMilitaryCalisthenics does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- All information you enter (weight, height, age, sex, fitness level, goal, weight history, and your generated training plan) is stored **only on your device**, using Android's local `DataStore`.
- Nothing is sent to any server. The app has no backend and does not require an internet connection to function.
- There is no account, sign-in, or user identification of any kind.
- The app includes no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
- Workout reminder notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device via Android's `WorkManager`; no data leaves your phone to make this work.

If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is removed with it.

If you have questions about this policy, reach out via [github.com/VidiPT89](https://github.com/VidiPT89/).
